# P7 v1.1: user feedback round (12 Nov 23:10 to 13 Nov 05:40 Dubai)

## What the user asked for, and what changed
- **Globe should look like Google Earth.**
  - The orbit view now uses NASA Blue Marble, with real clouds at orbit, fading into Esri imagery below about 4,500 km.
  - A soft light follows the camera, and the atmosphere is tuned.
  - The globe spins after 20 s idle at orbit (30 fps cap) and can be turned off.
- **Flights should zoom out to orbit, turn the globe, and zoom in.** One custom eased animation does this. It renders only while moving, any touch cancels it, and tiles are coarser while moving and sharp when still.
- **More detail.** Esri tiles to level 23 wherever Esri's tilemap says imagery exists. Elsewhere tiles stop at the native level and the "Beyond native imagery detail" chip shows.
- **Show current satellites.** Live CelesTrak positions for 182 Earth-observation satellites plus the ISS, with labels and next-orbit tracks for Landsat 8/9 and Sentinel-2A/B/C (SGP4 via satellite.js).
- **Search like Google Earth.** Type-ahead in the top bar (GeoNames via Open-Meteo, Photon backup, stories first). Choosing a result flies there and opens its place card.
- **Name a place and get stuff about it.** New explore mode: a Wikipedia card with photo, description and extract; "Worth a stop nearby", ranked by Wikipedia page views; a guide written by the local model, held to those facts.
- **Compare 2000 and 2001 of Palm Jumeirah: show the images and talk about it.** In-chat before/after slider, headline figure with dates and sensors, a narrated story streamed sentence by sentence, then the sourced context and downsides with numbered citations, evidence quality, and sources.
- **Chat didn't work with other places.** Fixed four ways:
  - lower-case or bare names now reach the geocoder;
  - English place names from Nominatim (`accept-language=en`, via geocode's own `_get` hook);
  - a heartbeat during live runs, so it never looks frozen;
  - a 150 s time-out with a friendly card.
- **Saved regions sometimes didn't work.** Cause: a click while another answer was running was silently ignored. Now a new question stops the old one.
- **Fonts and look.** IBM Plex Sans, Mono and Sans Arabic. The chat was rebuilt as a report: no bubbles, no card grid, hairline structure, citations. The layout is responsive: under 900 px the chat is a bottom sheet.
- **Backend must survive a burst.** Rate limits, gates and caches as described in the README. Load test: 3,000 visitors arriving 160 at a time, 21,000 requests in 36.8 s, 0 errors, story answer p95 253 ms.
- **Make it live (pre-computed, no model).** `Dockerfile` plus `render.yaml`. Demo mode was verified locally: no model, live measuring off, recorded narrations, explore and search working.

## Honesty guards added
- Narration sentences are dropped unless all their numbers AND capitalised names appear in the facts. This caught the 7B inventing "Machilipatnam" and "Kondapalli".
- Measurement sentences may not be credited to a news source; "according to The National" is stripped from them.
- An LLM-chosen story is dropped when the place doesn't match its aliases (Dubai Marina had been answered with Palm data).
- `explain_last` is accepted only from the rule router.
- Wikipedia articles must lie within 80 km of the place and must not be administrative pages.

## Security review findings (automated) and fixes
- **Narration cache poisoning through a client-supplied search hint.** Place narrations are now keyed by the resolved article and coordinates, and kept in memory only.
- **Unbounded narration file.** Only story narrations (`presets:` analyses) are persisted, capped at 200.
- **Spoofable `X-Forwarded-For`.** It is trusted only with `TRUST_PROXY=1`, and then only the last hop.
