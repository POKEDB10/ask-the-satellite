# P1 Shell, globe and LLM bake-off (21:35–21:55 Dubai)

## Key points
- `web/` is a new Cesium page. It has a 56 px glass top bar, a full-height globe plus chat grid (`--chat-w: clamp(380px,30vw,520px)`), dark only, and no build step.
- Libraries are vendored: Cesium 1.147.0, Inter and JetBrains Mono (woff2). The old Leaflet page is at `/legacy/`.
- Imagery stack: Natural Earth II (bundled) is the floor. Esri World Imagery sits on top, using `fromBasemapType(SATELLITE)` with the `.env` key or the keyless URL otherwise, with `maximumLevel` 19. Esri labels are off by default.
- Esri failure, meaning metadata fails or 3 tile errors in 15 s, hides Esri, shows a card and keeps Natural Earth.
- Quality dials are applied as specified. An FPS governor steps msaa, then resolutionScale, then SSE when the median FPS is under 30 for 5 s while the globe is moving.
- HUD shows coordinates, camera height, scale bar and a compass. It has zoom +/-, "Reset to orbit", the "Beyond native imagery detail" chip, auto-rotate with a fading hint, and a search pill.

## What works (tests run)
- Headless Chrome (playwright-core with system Chrome, D3D11) at 1280x720 and 1920x1080: no horizontal scroll, console clean, FPS 135 and 99.
- Visible Chrome on the RTX 4060 orbiting Palm at 250 m height for 7 min: 145 FPS median.
- Bake-off (`backend/bench_llm.py`, 10 planner questions × 3 runs, globe open on Palm at the same time):
  - qwen2.5:7b-instruct-q4_K_M: JSON 30/30, plan 27/30, p50 1.90 s, p95 2.43 s, interpretation 1.65 s, 4.6 GB, 100% GPU.
  - qwen3.5:9b-q4_K_M: JSON 30/30, plan 27/30, p50 2.51 s, p95 3.03 s, interpretation 1.59 s, 5.5 GB, 100% GPU.
  - qwen3.5:4b: JSON 30/30, plan 27/30, p50 1.34 s, p95 1.51 s, interpretation 0.81 s, 3.1 GB, 100% GPU.
- Choice: `LLM_MODEL=qwen2.5:7b-instruct-q4_K_M`, `LLM_FALLBACK=qwen3.5:4b`, `LLM_SMALL=qwen3.5:4b` (in `.env`).

## Deviations and why
- The 9B was not promoted. It passed the 100% GPU check, but it had the same accuracy, was slower, and leaves the least VRAM headroom beside Chrome on 8 GB. The research rule is "faster model that passes".
- The plan score counts the grounded plan, as `agent.plan()` uses it. The raw 7B output sets `intent=explain_last` too often; `_ground()` corrects it when there is no previous map.
- The EOX Sentinel-2 cloudless fallback is not built. Its licence for the 2018+ layers is non-commercial (CC BY-NC-SA, from memory) and I could not open s2maps.eu to confirm it. The prompt says to drop it in that case. The basemap choice is Esri or Natural Earth II, and Esri failure shows a card.
- The Esri attribution string is the one the service returns today: "Esri, Vantor, Earthstar Geographics, and the GIS User Community". Maxar is now Vantor.
- The in-app preview pane cannot capture WebGL, so `tools/shots.js` drives headless Chrome. `?shots` turns on `preserveDrawingBuffer` for capture only.
- The integrated-GPU pinning test was skipped: there was no contention to fix.
