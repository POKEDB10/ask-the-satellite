# P8 v1.2: surgical polish (13 Nov, 07:25 to about 08:30 Dubai)

## Files changed
- `web/globe.js`: sharper rendering (resolution scale up to 2, SSE 1.0 at all times, sibling preload, cache 1000), with a repaint on every `tileLoadProgressEvent`. Coarse tiles while moving were removed. The orbit textures fade from 9,000 to 3,000 km and are hidden below that. Flights and the orbit view are centred in the part of the canvas the chat leaves visible (`setInset`, `recenter`). EO satellite styling, a name label on click, orbit rings.
- `web/style.css`, `web/app.js`: the chat slides with `transform` only, over a canvas that never resizes. There is one repaint on `transitionend` (250 ms fallback), and the right-hand HUD slides with the chat. The selector bar (region, Date 1, Date 2, index, Run) syncs both ways. Report order: telemetry, swipe and thumbnails, field summary, named sources, next comparisons. "Geographic & Surface Context" and "Earth observation brief" headings.
- `web/index.html`: selector bar; "EO Satellites" button.
- `backend/change.py`: additive NDWI entry (bands green/NIR, threshold 0, labels). Nothing else in the engine changed.
- `backend/agent.py`: additive NDWI noun.
- `backend/extras.py`: `index` on the stream request (frame bank only); EO comparison chips on every explore card; no AI narration for Arabic; warm-up list now includes Aral Sea, Dubai, Kyoto, Burj Khalifa and Palm Jumeirah.
- `backend/narrate.py`: at most 3 sentences and 65 words; Earth observation brief and field-summary prompts; the model sees only the net change; recorded briefs persist for a fixed list of demo places.
- `backend/place.py`: restaurants, hotels, bars and retail removed; geographic and hydrological features ranked first.
- `backend/ops.py`: EO mission list (prefix match) plus the CelesTrak weather group; RAM guard (24 MB budget, files over 1.5 MB skipped in production).
- `backend/cache/narration.json`: re-recorded with the new prompts (11 entries). `backend/cache/presets/aral_sea/`: new.
- `tests/test_v12.py`: new offline self-checks.

## Deviations
- **Palm years.** The selector lists the years that really exist: 2000, 2001, 2003, 2008, Jan 2018, Jan 2024. 2002 and 2004–2006 were never cached (P2: too cloudy, or no Landsat 5 scene), and synthetic years are not allowed.
- **Index choices.** MNDWI, NDVI and NDWI are offered for the Palm frame bank only. Single-pair stories keep their own index (MNDWI or the flood rule), because changing it would need a live re-download.
- **Aral Sea.** Real precompute, but only 33% of the box is valid (Landsat scenes don't cover the whole 2.6° box). It is LOW, the headline is hidden, and it has no narration, as the trust rules require. Its 120 m overlay is placed linearly in latitude, so it can be off by up to about 7 px (about 800 m) at the box edges.
- **EO list.** Landsat 7 is not in CelesTrak's active catalogue any more, so it is not drawn.
- **Arabic.** AI narration is off for Arabic: the names guard checks Latin script only, and the model mixed in English words.
