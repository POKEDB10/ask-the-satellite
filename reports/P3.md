# P3 Chat, choreography and swipe

## Key points
- The chat uses `POST /api/chat/stream` (SSE). The first event is `plan` with `fly_to` from the rule router. The flight starts within the same tick (the cached Palm path answered in 0.3 s end to end).
- Overlays and cards are revealed at 70% of the flight or on arrival, whichever comes first. They fade in over 600 ms.
- Swipe uses `SplitDirection.LEFT` for before and `RIGHT` for after and change. The mask is unsplit. `scene.splitPosition` is driven by a draggable or arrow-key handle, and the tags carry sensor, resolution and date.
- Message order: ticker (collapses to "Done in N s · Verify"), image strip, What happened (measured card plus sourced context plus milestone note), Data quality, "From named sources, not measured by satellite" (or the AI hypothesis when there is no curated context), Next chips.
- Image strip: the first click focuses the swipe; the second opens a lightbox with zoom, pan and PNG download.

## What works (tests run)
- Headline query, headless at both sizes: the flight lands on Palm, the 2000 (Landsat 7) | 2001 (Landsat 7) swipe shows, and the cards render. The "Construction began June 2001…; this window mostly predates it" note appears.
- `tests/contract_test.py` checks every number in the measured and quality cards against `analysis.stats` or the scene dates: PASS.
- Projection check: Palm overlay edges line up with the coastline in the Esri basemap in the Before | Today screenshot. The maths gives at most 0.5 m error, so no `layers_geo` was added.
