# P4 Trust UI, context and attribution

## Key points
- The badge, valid-pixel bar and flag chips come from `change.py` and `agent.quality_card`, restyled only. The masked layer turns on by default for MEDIUM and LOW (checked on Palm 2001→2008: MEDIUM, `cross_sensor`, mask on).
- Below 50% valid, the headline is replaced by "Too much cloud to measure reliably", and the ignored-area km² chip is hidden too.
- Rule 3: every card that carries a km² figure (measured and quality) now prints both dates and both sensors beside it.
- Verify modal shows, for each date: STAC ids, timestamp, sensor, catalogue and collection, the reflectance scale and offset actually applied (frame metadata, additive `scene_*.scale/offset` on compare analyses), mask rule, and candidate days. It also lists the index formula and threshold, grid and cos² correction, MMU, valid shares, confidence rules, flags, analysis id and all attributions.
- Curated Palm context (`backend/presets.json`) has 3 "what happened" lines, 6 downsides (2 tagged "Disputed by developer" with the developer's rebuttal) and 2 counterpoints. Each has a source chip.
