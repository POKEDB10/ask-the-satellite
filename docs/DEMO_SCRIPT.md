# Demo script: Ask the Satellite (under 3 minutes)

Read the quotes out loud, like you're talking to a friend. *Italic lines* are what you do on screen. Every prompt below was tested and opens the right story.

**Hands-free typing:** press **Alt+1** to **Alt+4** and the app types each question for you like a person would (about 4 seconds), then sends it. Say the lead-in line while it types. **Esc** stops it if you press the wrong one. Keep talking while the globe flies; don't wait in silence. Spoken, it's about 340 words, roughly 2½ minutes.

**Before you start:** open the site and hard-refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R). The chat should be open on the right. Click **Orbit** so the whole Earth is showing.

---

## Opening

> "Hi everyone, this is **Ask the Satellite**.
> You ask how a place on Earth has changed, and we answer with real satellite images.
> Let me show you four quick examples."

---

## 1. Palm Jumeirah: building land

> "Let's start in Dubai. I'll ask about the land reclamation of Palm Jumeirah, from 2001 to 2008."

*Press **Alt+1**. It types* `Show me the land reclamation of Palm Jumeirah from 2001 to 2008` *and sends it by itself.*

> "Watch the globe: it zooms out, turns, and flies straight to Dubai."

*When the slider appears on the map, drag it left and right.*

> "On the left is May 2001, and it's just open sea. On the right is May 2008, and the Palm is there.
> The app measured **8.59 square kilometres** of sea turned into land. That's about 1,200 football pitches."

*Point at "Medium confidence" in the chat.*

> "It also says how much to trust this: **Medium**, because the two photos came from different satellites."

*Click **Verify** at the top, wait 2 seconds, close it.*

> "And **Verify** shows the exact satellite images we used, so anyone can check."

---

## 2. Lake Mead: losing water

> "That was people *adding* land. Now let's look at nature *taking water away*. Lake Mead, in the US, went through a big drought."

*Press **Alt+2**. It types* `How much did Lake Mead shrink during the drought?` *and sends it.*

> "Between 2019 and 2022 the lake lost **47.6 square kilometres** of water, about 15 percent.
> And we checked it: our numbers are within about **2 percent** of published scientific figures."

*Point at the short summary under "What this shows".*

> "This summary is written by our AI, but it's **not allowed to make up numbers**. If it tries, that sentence gets deleted."

---

## 3. UAE flood: sudden water

> "Drought is slow. Floods happen overnight. Let's go to the flood here in the UAE in April 2024."

*Press **Alt+3**. It types* `Show me the UAE flood in April 2024` *and sends it.*

> "On the 16th of April the UAE had its heaviest rain in recorded history.
> The app found **6.6 square kilometres** of newly flooded land."

*Hover over "why these scenes?"*

> "Clouds are a big problem for satellites, so the app picks the clearest day for this exact area."

---

## 4. Los Angeles wildfire: burned land

> "And finally, fire. In January 2025, huge wildfires hit Los Angeles. Let's see how much land burned."

*Press **Alt+4**. It types* `How much land burned in the Los Angeles wildfire in 2025?` *and sends it.*

*Drag the slider; the blue area is the burn scar.*

> "Everything in blue is burned land. We measured **93.4 square kilometres** for the Palisades fire.
> The official number from California's fire agency is about 95. So from space, we got within about **2 percent**."

---

## Closing

*Click **Orbit** to go back to the whole Earth.*

> "So that's Ask the Satellite: four kinds of change, all measured from real satellite images.
> The satellite measures, the AI explains, and you can check every number. Thank you!"

---

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The prompt opens the wrong place | Click outside the chat and press **1** Palm, **2** UAE flood, **3** Lake Mead or **4** LA fire. Or open **Stories** at the top |
| No AI summary shows up | Skip that line and say: "Even without the AI, every number is still here." |
| The globe freezes or goes black | Click **Orbit**, or refresh the page |
| The website is slow to load | The free server was asleep; wait about a minute |
