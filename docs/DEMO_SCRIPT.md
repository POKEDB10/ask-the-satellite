# Demo script: Ask the Satellite (under 3 minutes)

**Bold** = what you do. Quotes = what you say, about 330 words in total. At a calm pace that's about 2 minutes 20 seconds of talking, plus roughly 30 seconds of the globe flying.

**Before you start:** open the site and hard-refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R). Make sure the chat panel is open, then click **Orbit**. If typing fails, use backup keys **1** Palm, **2** UAE flood, **3** Lake Mead, **4** LA fire (click outside the chat box first).

---

## Opening (0:00–0:10)

> "This is Ask the Satellite. You ask how any place on Earth has changed, and real satellite images answer, with the evidence behind every number."

---

## 1. Palm Jumeirah (0:10–0:55)

**Type** `Palm Jumeirah 2001 to 2008` and press Enter. While the globe flies:
> "It flies there like Google Earth."

**Drag the swipe on the globe.**
> "May 2001: open sea. May 2008: the Palm. We measured **8.59 square kilometres** of sea turned into land, about 1,200 football pitches."

**Point at "Medium confidence".**
> "It says Medium, even with no clouds, because the two images come from different satellites. The app tells you the limits of its own answer."

**Click Verify.**
> "And Verify shows the exact satellite scenes and formula, so anyone can reproduce this number."

**Close Verify.**

---

## 2. Lake Mead (0:55–1:30)

**Type** `Did Lake Mead shrink?` and press Enter.
> "Lake Mead lost **47.6 square kilometres** of water between 2019 and 2022, about 15%."

**Point at the summary under "What this shows".**
> "How do we know it's right? We're within about 2% of a published benchmark. This summary is written by our AI, but any sentence with a number or name we didn't measure is deleted. The AI explains; it never invents."

---

## 3. UAE flood (1:30–2:05)

**Type** `UAE flood April 2024` and press Enter.
> "April 2024: the UAE's heaviest rain since records began. Our flood rule found **6.6 square kilometres** of newly flooded land."

**Hover over "why these scenes?"**
> "Clouds are the enemy of satellites, so the app checks every candidate day and picks the clearest one for this exact area. It shows you that choice."

---

## 4. LA wildfire (2:05–2:45)

**Type** `Los Angeles wildfire 2025` and press Enter.
> "Same engine, different science. A burn index finds where healthy plants turned to scorched ground."

**Drag the swipe; the blue is the burn scar.**
> "We measured **93.4 square kilometres** burned by the Palisades fire. The official figure is about 95. That's within about 2%, from orbit."

---

## Closing (2:45–2:55)

**Click Orbit.**
> "Real satellite measurements, honest confidence, sources for every claim. The satellite measures, the AI explains, and you can verify every number."

---

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Prompt goes to the wrong place | Press the backup key (1–4), or open **Stories** |
| No AI summary | Skip that line: "Every number is still here, even without the AI." |
| Globe stuck or black | Click **Orbit** or refresh |
| Public site slow | The free server was asleep; wait a minute or use the local copy |
