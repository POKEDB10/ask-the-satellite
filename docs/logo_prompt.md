# Logo prompt: Ask the Satellite

Paste into any image model (Midjourney, Ideogram, DALL·E, Firefly). Ideogram and DALL·E handle the wordmark best. Generate the symbol on its own first, then the lock-up.

## Symbol

> Minimal geometric logo mark for "Ask the Satellite", an app that measures how places on Earth change from satellite imagery. A circle representing Earth seen from orbit, with the right half filled in a translucent teal (#06B6D4) to suggest a before/after split, crossed by one thin elliptical orbit line, and a small solid teal dot on the orbit at the upper right as the satellite. Flat vector, 2 px-style uniform strokes, no gradients, no glow, no 3D, no text, no stars, no sparkles. Teal #06B6D4 and off-white #E8ECF2 on a near-black navy background #0B0F19. Centred, generous padding, reads clearly at 16 px. Swiss, precise, NASA-instrument feel, not playful.

## Lock-up (symbol plus wordmark)

> Horizontal logo lock-up: the symbol above on the left, then the wordmark "Ask the Satellite" in a clean grotesque sans-serif similar to IBM Plex Sans SemiBold, sentence case, tight but readable letter-spacing, off-white #E8ECF2 on #0B0F19. Symbol height matches the cap height plus the descender. Flat vector, no gradient, no shadow, no tagline.

## Negative prompt (models that support one)

`gradient, glow, lens flare, 3d render, bevel, rocket, stars, sparkle, emoji, cartoon, mascot, photo, busy background, extra text, watermark`

## Notes

- The in-app favicon and the top-bar mark (`#i-logo` in `web/index.html`) already follow this design, so a generated logo will match the UI.
- Export a 1024 px PNG and an SVG; trace the SVG if the model gives only raster.
